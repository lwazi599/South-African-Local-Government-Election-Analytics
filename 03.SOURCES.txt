CPT Election 2026 — DATA SOURCES
Access date: 2026-10-05
Metro: City of Cape Town (CPT), Western Cape
Purpose: Real open datasets for municipal/local government election forecasting (Election Day 04 Nov 2026).
No fabricated data. All election vote/turnout figures originate from the Electoral Commission of South Africa (IEC) or clearly cited IEC-derived mirrors.

================================================================================
A. IEC OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION RESULTS (PRIMARY)
Portal: https://results.elections.org.za/home/Downloads/ME-Results
Event IDs used: 1091 = LGE 2021; 402 = LGE 2016; 197 = LGE 2011

Suggested merge keys:
  - municipality code: CPT
  - ward_id / WardID: 19100001 .. 19100116 (2021/2016); 19100001 .. 19100111 (2011; 111 wards)
  - election_year: 2011 | 2016 | 2021
  - party_name (string) for party-level joins
NOTE: Ward boundary/ID changes between election years require defensive alignment before compare/predict.

YES — Ward-level party votes:
  2021: 116 ward XLS (all parties) + municipal summary
  2016: 116 ward XLS (all parties) + municipal summary + Open Elections wide CSV
  2011: 111 ward XLS (all parties) + municipal summary

YES — Turnout / registration by ward:
  2021, 2016, 2011 official IEC Voter Turnout XLS/PDF (+ derived tidy CSVs)

YES — ≥2 related mergeable datasets:
  IEC results (ward_id, CPT, year) × Stats SA Census 2022 municipal indicators (muni_code=CPT)
  IEC turnout (ward_id) × IEC ward party results (ward_id)
  Optional: CCT wards attributes (WARD_NAME) × IEC ward serial

NOT obtained as bot-friendly bulk CSV from interactive ME-Results wizard UI (JS portal);
equivalent official static LGEPublicReports XLS/PDF paths were used successfully (no fabrication).

2011 note: fewer wards (111) than 2016/2021 (116) — treat as structural change, not missing downloads.

G. SUGGESTED MERGE RECIPE (FOR MODELLING)
1) Build long table from ward detailed XLS: [election_year, municipality=CPT, ward_id, party_name, ward_votes, pr_votes, total_votes]
2) Left-join turnout: on [election_year, ward_id] → registered_voters, turnout_votes, turnout_rate
3) Left-join Census CPT row: on municipality/muni_code=CPT (metro features constant across wards; or use as metro controls)
4) For cross-year ward compare: map 2011→2016→2021 with explicit boundary alignment strategy before forecasting 2026.
